# NotifEyes

## What it is

A two-sided optometry staffing marketplace. Practices post fill-in shifts; licensed optometrists ("ODs") draw geographic watch zones and get alerted within seconds when a matching shift posts. NotifEyes charges the practice a flat $10 match fee, captured only when the practice confirms the OD showed up; the practice pays the OD's wage directly.

## Users

- **Practice owners and schedulers** — small-business optometry practices, often a single owner-operator who also sees patients. They post a shift when a doctor calls out or the schedule has a gap. State of mind: time-pressed, mildly stressed, needs reliability signals fast.
- **Fill-in ODs** — licensed optometrists picking up extra shifts around a home base. They set up watch zones once, then act on alerts. They check the dashboard between patients during the day, on a laptop, in bright clinical lighting.

## Core loop

Practice posts shift → watch-zone fanout notifies matching ODs (email is the hero channel, in-app + SMS secondary) → OD applies → practice books → attendance confirmed → $10 fee captured → blind reviews both ways.

## Tone

Trustworthy, warm-professional. This is a tool for clinicians and small-business owners handling real money and real schedules: friendly enough to feel human, never cutesy, never corporate-sterile. Copy is plain-language and calm ("wage paid directly by the practice", "no-show means no fee").

## Anti-references

- Gig-economy hustle aesthetics (surge-pricing reds, gamified streaks).
- Hospital-software sterility (gray data grids, dated enterprise chrome).
- Generic edu-SaaS clip art with no connection to optometry or geography.

## Strategic principles

- The map is the product's soul: watch zones are the differentiator, so geographic motifs are on-brand decoration.
- Speed of comprehension over density: an OD should grasp "anything for me today?" in two seconds.
- Trust is earned with verification badges, transparent fees, and calm states, not exclamation points.
